## Recording-disjoint assessment

Reserved 30 recording groups (194 clips, 158 negatives) solely for threshold calibration. These recordings are excluded from every fit and assessment fold.
The remaining 90 groups (628 clips) use 5-fold grouped CV. No recording appears on both sides of a split. Each head test fold uses a model fit on its training groups and a threshold selected using only the reserved calibration recordings.
The shipped pipeline is fitted on all development groups; its threshold is selected using the same reserved calibration recordings. Calibration recordings are not folded back into that fit.

Thresholds target empirical negative FPR ≤ 2.00% on calibration only. Assessment FPR may be higher. A small, correlated calibration sample does not establish a population or venue false-alarm guarantee; source-group counts matter more than the number of nearby clips.
Synthetic data validates plumbing and controlled discrimination only. Real contact recordings, venue background, and Pi 5 latency require separate validation. Clip-level FPR cannot establish false knocks or false confirmations per hour; use tools/eval_background.py on a long recording.
